Homepage photography and typography

The Three Deuces performance photograph (New York, circa August 1947) is by William P. Gottlieb, from the Library of Congress collection, now in the public domain. It shows Tommy Potter, Charlie Parker, Max Roach, Miles Davis and Duke Jordan. Original photograph and rights information. Homepage versions are cropped, resized and converted to WebP; the social preview is cropped to a landscape JPEG. Display contrast and cropping are applied with CSS.

The featured Ella Fitzgerald portrait uses the Carl Van Vechten public-domain photograph credited below. Its responsive versions are resized and converted to WebP.

Homepage fonts: Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans, distributed under the SIL Open Font License and served locally. Cormorant Garamond licence; DM Sans licence.
