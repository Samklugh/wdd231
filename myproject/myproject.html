Site name

Jazz Discoveries

I chose Jazz Discoveries because it describes the subject and the purpose of the site. The guide will help people who are new to jazz find musicians and recordings to listen to.

Site purpose

Jazz Discoveries will introduce visitors to jazz musicians and their music through approachable profiles, instruments, jazz styles and notable recordings. Visitors will be able to filter musicians, save their favourites and create a personal listening plan.

Target audience

The primary audience is people who are new to jazz and want a clear starting point. Casual listeners who recognise a musician or instrument but want to explore related styles and recordings are a secondary audience.

Three main pages

Home · index.html
Introduce the guide, explain how to begin, preview a few musicians and jazz styles, and direct visitors to the musician catalogue and listening planner.
Musicians · musicians.html
Display at least 15 musician profiles from a local JSON file. Each profile will include a name, instrument, jazz style and notable recording, with a short biography and image where available. Filters will narrow the list by instrument, style and saved favourites. A Learn more button will open an accessible profile dialog.
Listening Planner · listening-planner.html
Help visitors turn saved favourites into a listening plan. A form will collect a plan name, selected musicians or recordings, a listening date and optional notes. Visitors will be able to save their plan in this browser.

Planned functionality

  • Fetch local JSON asynchronously, parse it and show a useful message if loading fails. Use ES modules to organise the scripts.
  • Generate musician cards from the data, using array methods to filter results and template literals to assemble text.
  • Store favourite musician IDs and the listening plan in localStorage. Provide clear save and remove controls and handle unavailable storage.
  • Use a native modal dialog for musician details, with a visible Close button, Escape support and restored keyboard focus.
  • Submit the listening form to a separate confirmation page that displays the entered data. This supporting page will not count as one of the three main pages.

The finished site will use original HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no third-party framework. It will have responsive navigation, optimised images, source attributions and page transfers below 500 kB. Recordings will be recommendations with credited external links; audio will not autoplay.

Scenarios

  1. “I’m new to jazz. Which musicians and recordings should I start with?”

    The Home page will explain how to begin, and musician profiles will identify notable recordings to try.

  2. “I enjoy the saxophone. How can I find saxophonists and compare their jazz styles?”

    The Musicians page will offer instrument and style filters, with both properties visible on each profile.

  3. “How can I keep the musicians I like and decide what to listen to next?”

    Favourite controls will save musicians locally, and the Listening Planner will let visitors choose recordings and a listening date.

  4. “Can I learn more about a musician without losing my filtered results?”

    A profile dialog will reveal additional information while preserving the catalogue’s current filters.

Colour scheme

The palette combines a dark ink colour, warm paper and a copper accent. These are the only colours used to style this plan and will carry into the finished site.

Midnight ink

#142D3B

Body text, headings, navigation, borders and the main header background.

Warm paper

#F7F3E8

Page backgrounds and light text on dark header areas and accent buttons.

Copper

#9B431D

Links, section labels, important actions and favourite controls. Saved states will also have text labels.

Text must meet WCAG AA contrast requirements. Information and saved states will always include words or symbols so that meaning does not depend on colour alone.

Typography

Georgia · headings

Jazz musicians and their music

Georgia will be used for the site name, page headings, section headings and musician names. Its serif letterforms give the guide an editorial character. The generic serif family is the fallback.

Arial · body and controls

Explore musicians by instrument and style, save a favourite, and build your next listening session.

Arial will be used for paragraphs, navigation, buttons, form labels and supporting details. The generic sans-serif family is the fallback. Body text will start at 16px with a line height of 1.65.

Both selections are system fonts, so this plan needs no external font downloads. All text on this page uses these two font stacks.

Small viewport sketch · 320–640px

  1. A compact header with Jazz Discoveries and a menu button. The expanded menu contains Home, Musicians and Listening Planner.

  3. A featured-musicians section with profile previews stacked vertically.
  4. A brief introduction to jazz styles, followed by a link to the full catalogue.
  5. A listening-plan invitation and a footer with attribution and project-video links.

Large viewport sketch · desktop or tablet

  1. A header with the site name on the left and three horizontal navigation links on the right.
  2. An introduction with text and the main action beside a jazz-related image.
  3. Three featured-musician previews arranged in a row.
  4. A jazz-styles overview and a listening-plan invitation in adjacent sections.
  5. A full-width footer with attribution and project-video links.

Draw boxes, labels and navigation controls without decorative colour or detailed artwork.
